CANON EPISTEMOLOGY & LORE HIERARCHY

How do we scale a character when the author's tweet contradicts the comic book panel? What if a villain claims they are omnipotent, but the narrator says otherwise? This index defines the precise differences between Author Intent, Narrative Portrayal, and Character Statements, outlining the exact rules for prioritizing evidence.

The 5 Tiers of Epistemic Authority

1. Author Intent (Word of God)

External Lore / Q&A

What the real-world creator intended to convey, often revealed in interviews, tweets, or databooks. It holds supreme authority over ambiguous cosmology, but can be entirely discarded if it introduces blatant plot holes or directly contradicts explicitly drawn on-panel events (triggering the "Death of the Author" fallacy).

2. Omniscient Narrative

Objective Reality

The objective voice of the story—usually presented as omniscient narrator boxes, objective lore dumps, or overarching setting rules. Unlike character dialogue, the narrator cannot lie or be arrogant. It represents the indisputable, empirical truth of the cosmology as it unfolds on the page or screen.

3. Narrative Portrayal

Subtext & Function

How the story structurally treats a character. Even if a cosmic god lacks flashy on-screen explosive feats, if the overarching plot, tension, and narrative structure consistently portray them as an unstoppable, multiversal threat that lesser gods fear, their portrayal validates their high-tier status over visual limitations.

4. In-Universe Hypothesis

Educated Guesses

Theories proposed by scientists, scholars, or ancient texts *within* the setting. While highly informative for world-building, they remain hypotheses. They are accepted as true unless an empirical feat or omniscient narrator later proves the in-universe scholars were wrong or dealing with incomplete data.

5. Character Statements

Fallible Perspective

Dialogue spoken by actors inside the story. This is the weakest form of evidence because characters can be arrogant, ignorant, deceitful, or prone to hyperbole ("My sword can cut the universe!"). It requires corroboration from feats, portrayal, or the narrator to be accepted as literal fact.

The Conflict Resolution Matrix

When Evidence Clashes: The Rules of Prioritization

Author Intent
vs.
On-Panel Feats

Rule: Feats overrule intent if they blatantly contradict.
Example Scenario: The author tweets that "Character X is absolutely omnipotent and transcends reality." However, in the comic, Character X is defeated by a standard poisoned arrow and struggles to lift a car. By the rule of Death of the Author, the physical anti-feats shown in the primary canon overrule the external author statement.

Narrative Portrayal
vs.
Outlier Anti-Feats

Rule: Consistent Portrayal overrules isolated PIS (Plot-Induced Stupidity).
Example Scenario: A cosmic deity has consistently destroyed galaxies and is treated by the overarching narrative as the ultimate universal threat. In one specific comedic or poorly-written scene, they trip on a rock and get scratched by a house cat. Narrative Portrayal demands we treat the cat scratch as a non-canon outlier/gag, rather than downgrading the god to Street Level.

Omniscient Narrator
vs.
Character Hypothesis

Rule: The Narrator's objective truth overrules in-universe assumptions.
Example Scenario: A genius in-universe scientist hypothesizes that the approaching alien mothership can be destroyed by an EMP. However, a narrator box explicitly states: "The vessel was forged outside of time, immune to all electromagnetic forces." We immediately disregard the scientist's hypothesis, as the Narrator holds absolute epistemic authority over fallible characters.

Core Distinctions: Philosophical vs. Mathematical vs. Relative Physical Scaling

A. Physical & Relative Calc Scaling Tier 10 to 3-A + In-Verse Gaps

Above-4D Relative Physics: Even above 4D (in 5D–11D+ realms), relative physics still applies! Scalers can calculate in-verse power gaps using relative size ratios, momentum, or how much force one higher-dimensional being exerts to pin, overpower, or control an opponent.

B. Mathematical Scaling Low 2-C up to High 1-B / Low 1-A

Domain: Numerical logic, coordinate geometry ($\mathbb{R}^n$), Hilbert spaces, and set-theoretic cardinalities ($\aleph_0, \aleph_1, \Omega$). Math holds power over physics because it extrapolates and formalizes physical events that occur naturally.

Contingent Limitation: Mathematical laws are formal axioms that are contingent on math itself existing. Because math is a specialized subset of logic restricted to quantity, extension, and multiplicity, no amount of stacking numbers or dimensions can ever escape quantitative logic.

C. Philosophical Scaling Baseline 1-A up to Tier 0

Domain: Explaining and governing what is fundamentally non-physical and non-quantitative. Philosophy is required for Metaphysics to be understood and for Mathematical axioms to even be formulated or hold truth-value.

The 1-A Transition: Outerversal (Tier 1-A) is the exact point where you transition from Math to Philosophy. Once an entity transcends all dimensional coordinates and set-theoretic quantities, only philosophical ontology (Platonic Forms, Modal Realism, Apophatic Monism) can scale them.

Mandatory Epistemic Rule Before Scaling

Nominal Myth-Borrowing vs. True Theological & Conceptual Integration

A foundational rule in powerscaling is that borrowing the names, titles, iconography, or surface-level narratives of religious figures and mythological pantheons does NOT automatically import the real-world philosophical cosmology or tiering of that tradition. Fictional franchises must be scaled strictly by what they demonstrate and conceptualize within their own canonical text, never by external name-association (the Nominal Fallacy).

Type A: Franchise Copying Myth / Surface Adaptation 0 Automatic Upscale

A franchise may copy the names of supreme deities, angels, Buddhas, or primordial titans, and even adapt their famous mythological battles word-for-word, yet strip away the underlying metaphysical ontology. If a fictional deity bearing a sacred name is bound by physical planetary geography, relies on finite stamina, bleeds from conventional kinetic weaponry, and lacks the apophatic non-duality or transfinite recursion of the source text, they scale strictly to their on-screen physical feats (e.g., Tier 7 to Tier 4). Name-dropping "Nirvana," "Wuji," or "Omnipotence" as a combat technique name grants zero cosmological elevation.

Type B: Franchise Conceptualizing Theology & Ontology Valid High-Tier Scaling

Conversely, when a franchise genuinely conceptualizes the philosophical architecture of a tradition—explicitly depicting the illusory nature of spatial-temporal phenomena, recursive holographic containment across infinite worlds, complete coordinate divorce prior to duality, or absolute divine simplicity (uncaused aseity)—the cosmology earns its corresponding tier (Tier 1-B, Tier 1-A, or Tier 0). Here, the tier is awarded not because of the religious label, but because the work substantively establishes the ontological axioms in its world-building.
